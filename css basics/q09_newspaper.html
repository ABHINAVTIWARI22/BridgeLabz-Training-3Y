The Daily Times: City Celebrates Science Fair

Hundreds of students gathered at the city hall on Sunday to present their science projects. The event showed creativity and hard work from every school in the district.

Judges praised the projects on clean energy, robotics and water saving. Many young innovators said they were inspired by their teachers and families who supported them throughout the year.

Local leaders announced that the fair will now be held every year with a larger prize pool. Organisers hope this will encourage even more children to join in and explore science.

Parents shared that the fair gave their children confidence. Visitors walked around the stalls for hours, asking questions and learning how each model worked in simple words.

The day ended with a prize ceremony and a cultural programme. Everyone left the hall with smiles and new ideas to try at home or at school in the coming weeks.
